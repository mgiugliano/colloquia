# Hands-on Part

Brainstorming session within the groups, about problems Medtronics faced/is facing. 

Thinking about those real-world and very concrete problems is a valuable exercise concerning the design process. 


For instance:

- How to synchronize chronic recordings recorded via implantable devices (i.e., no physical I/O port) with external signals (EEG, IMU's, etc) for research purpose?

- How to identify and deal with artifacts while sensing and adapting stimulation? 


Sample references: papers 3, 4, and 5.
